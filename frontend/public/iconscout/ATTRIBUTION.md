## Replacing with IconScout downloads

1. Recolor in [IconScout SVG Editor](https://iconscout.com/) to `#8b7ff4`.
2. Export Lottie as JSON; keep filenames above.
3. Add row: pack name, asset URL, download date.

Example:

```
- Pack: SaaS Animation Pack
- URL: https://iconscout.com/lottie-animation/...
- Downloaded: 2026-05-22
```
